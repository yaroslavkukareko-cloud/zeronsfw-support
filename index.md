## FAQ

### How do I turn protection On/Off?
Open the ZeroNSFW popup and press the main power button.
If sites are still blocked, also check **Site Blocking** settings (see troubleshooting below).

### What do Light / Normal / Strict modes mean?
Modes control how sensitive the image filter is:
- **Light**: fewer false positives, less aggressive blocking
- **Normal**: balanced (recommended)
- **Strict**: maximum filtering, may hide more borderline content

### Why are images hidden first?
ZeroNSFW works preventively: images can be hidden before they are shown, then revealed after local analysis.
This reduces the chance of “unsafe” images flashing on screen.

### Does ZeroNSFW upload images or browsing data anywhere?
No. Image analysis is performed locally in your browser.  
ZeroNSFW does not send image contents, browsing history, search queries, or analysis results to the developer.

## Troubleshooting

### A site is blocked even when Protection is Off
Protection On/Off controls the **image filter**.  
If a website is blocked at the page level, it is usually due to **Site Blocking** rules.

Try this:
1) Open ZeroNSFW popup → go to **Site Blocking** (or the Sites screen, if available).
2) Disable **Site Blocking** (or disable **Auto-block** / “Auto-block porn”).
3) Reload the page.

### Search pages are blocked (Google / Yandex)
This is controlled by the search query blocking feature.
Disable **Auto-block** (or the related search blocking setting) in Site Blocking settings, then reload the search page.

### Pages load slowly or too many images stay hidden
Try:
1) Switch mode from **Strict → Normal** (or **Light**).
2) Reload the page.
3) If the issue persists on a specific site, send a bug report (see above).

### The extension seems not to work at all
Check:
1) The extension is enabled in `chrome://extensions`
2) The site is not in your allow/block lists unexpectedly
3) Reload the page (some sites are heavy SPAs and need a refresh)
4) If still broken, email us a bug report
